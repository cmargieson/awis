Need an AWIS number before departure? AWIS Phonebook puts Automatic Weather Information Service phone numbers for Australian aerodromes in your pocket — searchable by aerodrome name or ICAO code, ready to dial in one tap.

Built for GA pilots, instructors, and dispatchers who want a fast reference on the ramp, in the briefing room, or on the way to the airport. No account, no ads, and no internet connection required once installed.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Search by aerodrome name (e.g. Sydney) or ICAO identifier (e.g. YSSY)
2. Tap the result you need
3. Your phone opens the dialer with the AWIS number ready to call

FEATURES
• 223 aerodromes across all Australian states and territories
• Instant search as you type
• One-tap dial — no copying numbers by hand
• Fully offline — the directory is bundled in the app
• Light and dark mode — follows your device setting
• Private by design — no analytics, tracking, or data collection

COVERAGE
Includes major and regional aerodromes nationwide, from capital-city fields to remote strips. Each entry shows the aerodrome name, ICAO code, and AWIS phone number.

PRIVACY
AWIS Phonebook does not require sign-in, does not collect personal data, and does not connect to any backend service. Your searches stay on your device.

IMPORTANT
AWIS Phonebook is a convenience directory only. Always verify phone numbers and weather information through official sources before flight. The developer is not responsible for outdated numbers or service availability.
