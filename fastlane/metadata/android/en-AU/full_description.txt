AWIS Phonebook helps pilots and aviation enthusiasts quickly find Automatic Weather Information Service (AWIS) phone numbers for aerodromes across Australia.

Search by aerodrome name or ICAO identifier, then tap a result to open your phone dialer. The full directory is bundled in the app, so it works offline with no account and no internet connection required.

Features:
• Search ~220 Australian aerodromes by name or ICAO code
• One tap to dial the AWIS phone number
• Works fully offline — no backend, no tracking, no ads
• Light and dark mode support

Built for quick reference on the ramp or in the cockpit when you need an AWIS number fast.
